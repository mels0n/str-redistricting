# Maps changelog

## 3 (2026-10-13)

- Every State House, State Senate and Federal House map redrawn with engine 2.0.0.
- Hawaii's State House is the one map whose cuts change: no cut now splits Oahu from Kauai at the island link, and its range falls from 4,757 people (16.7%) to 158 (0.55%). Every other map is cut exactly as before.
- Balancing now also makes moves that keep two districts exactly as close to equal while shortening the border, so every map with more than one district has slightly different borders. Ranges stay within a few people of what they were.
- One-district maps are unchanged.

## 2 (2026-10-11)

- Added State House and State Senate maps for every state that has the chamber (Nebraska has no State House).
- Federal House maps rebuilt; every one is unchanged.

## 1 (2026-10-11)

- First release: congressional districts for all 50 states, drawn by engine 1.0.0 from the 2020 Census.
